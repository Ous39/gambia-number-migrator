# User Guide

## Scan contacts

Open the app and tap **Scan Contacts**. The app requests contact permission and processes contacts locally.

## Preview changes

Tap **Preview Changes** to see original number, normalized old number, migrated number, operator, confidence, rule, and status.

## Add new numbers

During the transition period, choose **Add New Numbers**. This adds the new 9-digit number under the same contact and keeps the old 7-digit number.

## Replace old numbers

Use **Replace Old Numbers** only when you intentionally want to remove old numbers. The app shows a warning during the transition period.

## Remove old duplicates

After transition, use **Remove Old Duplicates**. The app only removes an old number when the matching new 9-digit number already exists in the same contact and is verified by rules.

## Restore backup

Open **Backup / Restore** to see local backups. Backups are created before add, replace, and cleanup operations.
