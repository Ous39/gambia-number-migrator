## Description

Gambia Number Migrator is a privacy-first utility for reviewing and updating saved Gambian phone numbers during the numbering transition.

The app scans contacts on your device, shows every proposed change and lets you decide exactly what to update. Create a local backup before migration, keep original numbers during the transition and later remove only verified old duplicates.

Features include private scanning, selective migration, local backup and restore, preview and confirmation, migration history, notifications, accessible light and dark themes, and configurable support contacts.

Contact names and phonebook contents remain on your device and are not uploaded to the administration service.
