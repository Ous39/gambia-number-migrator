# Google Play Store Listing

## App name

Gambia Number Migrator

## Short description

Privately scan, preview, back up and migrate Gambian contact numbers.

## Full description

Gambia Number Migrator helps people safely update saved Gambian phone numbers during the national numbering transition.

Scan contacts privately on your device, review every proposed change, create a local backup and choose which numbers to update. The recommended Add & Keep Old mode adds the new number while preserving the original during the transition period. Cleanup tools can later remove verified old duplicates only when the corresponding new number exists in the same contact.

Key features:

- Private on-device contact scanning
- Clear preview before any contact is changed
- Local backup and restore
- Selective migration by contact or operator
- Safe Add & Keep Old mode
- Verified old-number cleanup
- Migration history and status notifications
- Light and dark appearance
- Support through email, phone or WhatsApp when configured

Privacy is central to the app. Contact names and phonebook contents are processed locally and are not uploaded to the administration service. Only limited operational information, such as an anonymous device reference, payment status and notification token, may be sent when those services are enabled.

Always review proposed changes and keep a backup before updating contacts.
